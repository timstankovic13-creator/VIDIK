# RC3 Execution Plans — Cases 004–014

This layer instantiates the same execution-control machinery used for Cases 003, 011 and 012 across Cases 004–014.

## Rule

Instantiation is not evidence acquisition and is not a recommendation. Every case starts `BLOCKED` until all six execution-readiness gates are satisfied: complete contract, authorized allocation, actual exposure, admissible evidence, defensible counterfactual, and measurement readiness.

## Evidence discipline

No total-program budget may be treated as a marginal exposure. No simple before/after change may be promoted to a causal effect. Population mismatch, unresolved evidence conflict, staleness, post-boundary evidence used for historical decisions, or an unmeasured counterfactual keeps the case blocked.

## Execution sequence

`PLAN → AUTHORIZE → RECORD ALLOCATION → RECORD ACTUAL EXPOSURE → VERIFY EVIDENCE/COUNTERFACTUAL → FREEZE PREDICTION → ESTIMATE EFFECT → ADVERSARIAL REVIEW → LEARN`

The historical decision boundary remains `2023-12-06`; current evidence is learning-plane evidence unless separately shown admissible for the historical decision.
