# VIDIK UX Audit v1

## Audit goals
Evaluate whether a first-time municipal decision-maker can understand the purpose, recommendation, controls and evidence path quickly without sacrificing transparency.

## Review sequence
1. First impression / 30-second comprehension
2. City selection and context
3. Resource and risk controls
4. Recommendation hierarchy
5. Evidence and provenance disclosure
6. Why / Why-not
7. Uncertainty and VOI
8. Outcome learning
9. Mobile layout and critical controls
10. Accessibility: keyboard, focus, labels, contrast, semantic structure

## Design principles
- Answer first; progressive disclosure for depth.
- Decision transparency must remain prominent.
- Evidence should be understandable without flattening uncertainty.
- Avoid institutional visual clutter.
- Never make decorative visuals imply evidence.

## Friend review prompts
What is confusing? What feels outdated or institutional? What would you surface/hide? Does the recommendation feel trustworthy? Can you find the evidence and Why-not reasoning? Does it work naturally on mobile?
