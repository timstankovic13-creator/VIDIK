# Case 001 — Real Ottawa Marginal Chain Audit

**Decision boundary:** 2023-12-06

## Finding

The historical evidence now supports a **real, Ottawa-specific partial chain** for supportive housing, but it still does **not** support a defensible marginal allocation chain.

### Observed evidence available before the boundary

Ottawa's 2023 Housing and Homelessness Progress Report records:

- **$39,390 average annual operating funding per supportive-housing unit**.
- **78%** of people remained housed for **1+ year**.
- **56%** remained housed for **2+ years**.
- Supportive housing is described as housing combined with on-site supports including case management, referrals, advocacy, health services and substance-use supports.
- In 2023, **57 new supportive units were completed**, and **54 supportive units were funded**.

These figures are genuine Ottawa observations, not synthetic test values.

## What can be calculated — descriptively only

Using the reported average annual operating funding per unit:

**$1,000,000 / $39,390 = 25.39 units-equivalent of annual operating funding**.

Applying the reported 78% one-year retention descriptively gives:

**25.39 × 0.78 = 19.80 unit-years retained for 1+ year**.

These calculations are useful diagnostic quantities, but they are **not admitted as a marginal production function**.

## Why the chain still fails the marginal gate

1. `$39,390` is explicitly an **average annual operating funding per unit**, not an identified marginal cost of the next unit.
2. The 78% retention statistic is an observed program outcome, not a causal estimate of the incremental effect of an additional $1M allocation.
3. The evidence does not establish that another $1M would purchase 25.39 additional units under the same conditions.
4. The evidence does not establish the activity/eligibility denominator required for a marginal allocation model.
5. No admissible Ottawa-specific causal estimate connects an incremental supported placement to a serious-harm/system outcome.
6. Therefore the chain cannot honestly be promoted to `CAD -> marginal capacity -> activity -> outcome -> serious-harm impact`.

## Classification

| Layer | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Ottawa resource context | observed | $39,390 average annual operating funding/unit |
| Capacity | observed-average | 57 completed / 54 funded units, but no marginal conversion |
| Activity | partial | supportive-housing provision is documented |
| Housing outcome | observed | 78% retained 1+ year; 56% 2+ years |
| Marginal causal effect | missing | no incremental allocation estimate |
| Serious-harm/system effect | missing | no admissible Ottawa-specific bridge |
| Full marginal chain | **BLOCKED** | fail closed |

## Decision

**Do not manufacture a recommendation.**

The new reconstruction layer should preserve the observed figures as evidence while refusing to convert them into an Ottawa marginal effect. Case 001 therefore remains **NO RECOMMENDATION — BLOCKED**.

## Next evidence target

The highest-value remaining search is for a pre-boundary Ottawa or Ontario program evaluation / contract / funding decision that identifies both:

1. incremental operating or capital resource change and resulting incremental service capacity; and
2. an outcome comparison attributable to that increment.

A serious-harm bridge should be sought separately. If neither exists at the boundary, the correct endpoint is a formally documented evidence insufficiency, not an inferred recommendation.
