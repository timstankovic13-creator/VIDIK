# RC3 Case 002 — Ottawa ASE Experiment Execution

## Execution status
**BLOCKED — NO EXECUTABLE EXPERIMENT / NO RECOMMENDATION**

This is the first RC3 experiment execution against a real municipal case. The purpose is to prove that VIDIK can take a candidate through the experiment contract and stop when the evidence needed to make the experiment causal and decision-eligible is absent.

## Question frozen before current-learning review
Should Ottawa allocate a marginal municipal resource unit to incremental automated speed enforcement, and if so at what deployment configuration, to maximize serious road-safety benefit relative to the status quo and alternatives?

## Evidence result
The existing Case 002 dossier establishes:
- a real pre-boundary ASE program and planned activity;
- candidate selection criteria;
- a historical resource/capacity chain only at partial resolution;
- no admissible candidate-specific marginal price/exposure for an additional camera/deployment package;
- no admissible candidate-specific causal estimate linking that marginal exposure to a system/serious-harm outcome.

The City subsequently reported strong changes in speed compliance and high-end speeding at original pilot sites, and later reported deterioration after cameras were removed. Those observations are **current-learning / validation evidence only** for this experiment and are not allowed to rewrite the 2023 decision. The City's later material also identifies concurrent road-safety measures, so the removal observations do not by themselves establish an isolated ASE causal effect.

## RC3 contract gate
A complete executable prediction freeze cannot honestly be created from the available evidence because the marginal intervention exposure and defensible serious-harm counterfactual are not established. Filling those fields with a synthetic cost, effect, or ROI would violate the RC3 fail-closed rule.

### Required before an executable ASE experiment could exist
1. A lawful, prospectively available ASE intervention or a legally valid successor intervention.
2. A defensible marginal resource unit and actual allocation ledger.
3. Pre-specified treatment and comparison sites or another credible causal design.
4. Pre-intervention speed, high-end speeding, collision and serious-injury baselines.
5. A pre-registered primary outcome and success threshold.
6. Spillover/displacement measurement.
7. Stop/rollback/reallocation rules.

## Current-learning validation (not a recommendation)
The City reports that at original pilot sites compliance increased from 16% before cameras to 57% within three months, 69% after one year and 81% after three years; high-end speeding fell from 14% to 4%, 2% and 0.7%. The City also reports that after ASE removal, compliance decreased and high-end speeding increased at former sites. These are useful for hypothesis generation and future evaluation design, but they are not promoted to a historical marginal causal effect.

## Result
**RC3 passes by refusing to manufacture the missing experiment.** Case 002 remains historically frozen as `NO RECOMMENDATION — BLOCKED` and is not model-eligible.
