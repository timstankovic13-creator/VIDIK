# VIDIK Evidence Hierarchy

| Level | Meaning | Permitted claim |
|---|---|---|
| A | Direct municipal measurement | Descriptive claim about the measured record |
| B | High-quality external evidence | Evidence-informed parameter/claim with applicability assessment |
| C | Transported evidence | Exploratory or qualified parameter with transportability assessment |
| D | Observational/limited evidence | Qualified, uncertainty-explicit claim |
| E | Assumption/exploratory | Scenario analysis only; must not be represented as established fact |

Evidence quality, provenance, temporal validity, transportability, conflict, staleness, and uncertainty must remain visible in the Decision Object.
