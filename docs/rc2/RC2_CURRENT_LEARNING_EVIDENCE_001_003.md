# RC2 Current-Learning Evidence Pack — Cases 001–003

**Historical boundary:** `2023-12-06`
**Purpose:** record post-boundary evidence for current learning / validation without altering the RC1 historical freeze.

## Case 001 — Ottawa Housing First

### Evidence observed

- Ottawa's current Housing and Homelessness reporting states that Housing First is a core component of the City's plan and describes it as prioritizing people experiencing homelessness for housing and supports. The City also states that providing housing with supports leads to better quality of life and is less costly than remaining in emergency shelter.
- The 2024 progress reporting records **398 households supported through the Housing First Program**, alongside other housing-system outcomes. This is a program/system observation, not by itself a marginal causal estimate for an incremental Housing First dollar or FTE.
- The 2025 progress reporting records **388 households housed through Housing First** and other system-level housing outcomes.

### RC2 chain assessment

| Stage | Current-learning status | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| resource | PARTIAL | program resource exposure must be linked to the specific marginal unit |
| capacity | PARTIAL | program capacity/exposure denominator required |
| activity | PRESENT | households supported/housed are reported |
| outcome | PARTIAL | housing placement is observable, but sustained-housing and comparison outcomes require extraction |
| systemOutcome | PRESENT/PARTIAL | broader housing-system outcomes are reported, but attribution to Housing First is not established |
| seriousHarm | INCOMPLETE | no candidate-specific serious-harm pathway established |

**Disposition:** current-learning evidence exists; **not recommendation-grade**.

## Case 002 — Automated Speed Enforcement

### Evidence observed

Ottawa's ASE evaluation reports that at the original eight pilot sites, posted-speed compliance increased from **16% before implementation to 57% within three months, 69% after one year, and 81% after three years**. High-end speeding fell from **14% to 4%, 2%, and 0.7%** over the same intervals.

The City also reports that its ASE data are collected at camera sites and used to track program effectiveness. Later reporting indicates that removal of cameras was associated with reduced posted-speed compliance and increased high-end speeding at former ASE sites, while noting that other road-safety measures complicate interpretation.

### RC2 chain assessment

| Stage | Current-learning status | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| resource | PARTIAL | operating/capital cost per marginal camera/site must be extracted |
| capacity | PRESENT | camera deployment/site exposure is observable |
| activity | PRESENT | camera enforcement and speed observations are measurable |
| outcome | PRESENT | speed-limit compliance and high-end speeding are directly measured |
| systemOutcome | PARTIAL | road-safety system effects require collision linkage and attribution |
| seriousHarm | INCOMPLETE | collision injury/fatality pathway not yet candidate-specific enough for promotion |

**Disposition:** strongest current-learning outcome evidence of the first three cases, but **not recommendation-grade until marginal cost, collision/system effects, counterfactual and serious-harm linkage are completed**.

## Case 003 — Ottawa Paramedic Capacity

### Evidence observed

Ottawa's 2024 annual reporting states that the Paramedic Service met all legislated response-time targets for the first time since 2020 and that Level Zero events decreased by 79% in 2024. Later 2025 reporting states that Level Zero time fell by another 92% in 2025 and describes improved paramedic availability.

These are system-performance observations. They do not by themselves establish the marginal causal effect of a particular additional paramedic FTE, vehicle, or budget increment.

### RC2 chain assessment

| Stage | Current-learning status | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| resource | PARTIAL | marginal FTE/vehicle/resource exposure must be linked to the period |
| capacity | PRESENT | availability / Level Zero measures provide capacity evidence |
| activity | PRESENT | response activity is measurable through service reporting |
| outcome | PRESENT/PARTIAL | response-time targets are reported; patient-level outcomes need extraction |
| systemOutcome | PRESENT | Level Zero and response-time system performance are reported |
| seriousHarm | PARTIAL | cardiac-arrest response outcomes are mentioned, but a full candidate-specific pathway is not yet assembled |

**Disposition:** substantial current-learning evidence; **not recommendation-grade until marginal exposure, counterfactual, causal attribution and serious-harm linkage are completed**.

## Cross-case governance result

These observations are deliberately assigned to the **CURRENT_LEARNING** plane. They must not be inserted into the 2023-12-06 historical recommendation record. A current decision using them would be a new decision context with its own as-of date, evidence snapshot and outcome-learning object.
