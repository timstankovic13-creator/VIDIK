## Required executable chain

Each case must establish, for the proposed marginal intervention:

`resource → capacity → activity → outcome → system outcome → serious-harm pathway`

The first three stages do not by themselves justify a recommendation. A system outcome must be linked to the candidate exposure, and the serious-harm pathway must be explicit rather than inferred from a convenient proxy.

## Temporal rule

Evidence published after `2023-12-06` can be valuable current-learning evidence. It cannot be inserted into the historical evidence plane or used to retroactively alter the RC1 freeze. If a current decision is eventually made, it receives its own as-of date, evidence snapshot, uncertainty state, and decision identity.

## Promotion gate

A candidate remains `NO RECOMMENDATION` until all of the following are evidenced:

1. exact marginal resource unit and incremental cost/exposure;
2. candidate-specific capacity/activity production relationship;
3. pre-specified counterfactual or credible comparison;
4. causal identification and attribution;
5. outcome plus system-outcome linkage;
6. serious-harm pathway;
7. transportability estimate to the proposed setting;
8. uncertainty distributions including correlated errors where relevant;
9. sensitivity/recommendation-flip analysis;
10. same-unit alternatives and opportunity cost;
11. reproducible evidence lineage and provenance;
12. immutable decision snapshot plus outcome-learning checkpoints.

## Result

Cases 004–012 remain blocked. This is a deliberate evidence ceiling, not a model failure. The next acquisition target is candidate-level marginal exposure and comparison data, not a fabricated benefit-per-dollar estimate.
