## Decision rule

A case can move beyond `NO RECOMMENDATION` only when its historical evidence is admissible and its candidate-specific causal chain, marginal exposure, counterfactual, attribution, transportability, uncertainty, alternatives, lineage, serious-harm pathway, legal authority, implementation feasibility, equity/distributional assessment, measurement contract, and lifecycle controls are all satisfied.

Later evidence belongs to the `CURRENT_LEARNING` plane and cannot rewrite the historical decision.

## Execution-governance requirements

Every future candidate promotion must additionally pass:

1. **Legal authority** — jurisdiction and effective dates established; restrictions fail closed.
2. **Implementation feasibility** — procurement, staffing, infrastructure, dependencies and timeframe assessed.
3. **Equity/distribution** — beneficiaries, burdens, geography, differential effects and trade-offs exposed.
4. **Measurement readiness** — metric, baseline, owner, frequency, denominator, missing-data and revision policies, and intervention sensitivity established.
5. **Implementation ≠ impact** — successful delivery/activity cannot be represented as causal impact without attribution.
6. **Decision lifecycle** — review/expiry/supersession/withdrawal/reauthorization are explicit and preserve the original decision hash.

## Current evidence boundary

The matrix intentionally does not convert later outcomes into historical recommendations. For example, Ottawa reports that ASE removal after provincial legislative change reduced posted-speed compliance at former camera sites, while also stating that the findings do not isolate ASE from other road-safety measures. Ottawa also reports a 92% reduction in Level Zero time in 2025, but system improvement does not by itself identify the marginal causal effect of one investment. Those are appropriate current-learning inputs, not retroactive proof of a 2023 recommendation. 

## RC2 conclusion

The **governance architecture is complete for RC2 close-out**. The substantive evidence universe remains fail-closed: all 001–014 historical recommendations remain `NO RECOMMENDATION` until their missing candidate-specific evidence is actually acquired and validated.
