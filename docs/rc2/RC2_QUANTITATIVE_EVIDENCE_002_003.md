# RC2 Quantitative Evidence Pack — Cases 002–003

**Historical boundary:** `2023-12-06`
**Evidence plane:** `CURRENT_LEARNING`
**Rule:** These measurements do not mutate the frozen RC1 historical decisions. They support a new current decision context and identify exactly what remains missing before a recommendation can be promoted.

## Case 002 — Automated Speed Enforcement

### Stable observations

| Measure | Observation | Evidence role |
|---|---:|---|
| Original pilot sites | 8 | exposure definition |
| Posted-speed compliance, pre-camera | 16% | baseline outcome |
| Posted-speed compliance, 3 months | 57% | outcome |
| Posted-speed compliance, 1 year | 69% | outcome |
| Posted-speed compliance, 3 years | 81% | outcome |
| High-end speeding, pre-camera | 14% | baseline outcome |
| High-end speeding, 3 years | 0.7% | outcome |
| Program fines since launch (through Sept. 2025) | $97.5M | financial context, not causal benefit |
| Program operating/admin cost (same memo) | $37.6M | cost context, not marginal camera cost |
| Road Safety Reserve allocation | $41.6M | downstream reinvestment context |

**Sources:** City of Ottawa ASE data-driven program; City memorandum on the ASE program (Oct. 2, 2025).

### Interpretation guard

The behavioural effects are large and repeatedly measured, but the available city-level summary does **not** establish the causal effect of one additional camera or one additional dollar on collisions or serious harm. The financial figures are program-level totals and cannot be divided by camera count to create a valid marginal cost without an allocation schedule and exposure denominator.

### Promotion status

`BLOCKED`

Missing promotion-grade fields:
- marginal resource unit and incremental cost;
- candidate-specific counterfactual / comparison sites;
- collision and serious-injury/fatality linkage attributable to the candidate exposure;
- transportability from pilot sites to the proposed marginal site;
- uncertainty model and correlated-error treatment;
- alternative road-safety intervention comparison on the same marginal unit.

## Case 003 — Paramedic Capacity

### Stable observations

| Measure | Observation | Evidence role |
|---|---:|---|
| Additional paramedic staff in 2023 budget proposal | 14 staff + vehicles | historical resource exposure |
| Paramedic staff added since 2023 (later City reporting) | 118 | current system capacity context |
| Level Zero time, four years ago | 73,000 minutes | baseline system outcome |
| Level Zero time, 2024 | 11,000 minutes | system outcome |
| Level Zero time, 2025 | <1,000 minutes | system outcome |
| 2025 Level Zero change | -92% | system outcome |
| Four-year Level Zero improvement | ~99% | system outcome |

**Sources:** City of Ottawa 2023 budget material; City of Ottawa 2026 paramedic annual-report update.

### Interpretation guard

The later system improvement is strong evidence that the service changed materially, but the reported figures combine staffing, deployment, dispatch, diversion, and other system changes. They cannot be attributed to the 2023 marginal staffing proposal without a candidate-level exposure timeline and counterfactual.

### Promotion status

`BLOCKED`

Missing promotion-grade fields:
- exact marginal FTE/vehicle exposure by period;
- staffing-to-capacity production relationship;
- counterfactual availability trajectory without the marginal resource;
- causal attribution separating staffing from dispatch/diversion/deployment changes;
- patient-level or serious-harm pathway linked to response performance;
- transportability across call type, geography and shift;
- uncertainty/correlation model and alternative-resource comparison.

## Cross-case quantitative conclusion

Cases 002 and 003 now have materially stronger quantitative current-learning evidence than the earlier screening pass. Neither clears the promotion gate. The correct next executable step is **not** to manufacture a recommendation; it is to obtain the missing marginal exposure and counterfactual data, then run the evidence-governed blind engine and adversarial stress harness.
