# RC2 Evidence Acquisition Queue — Cases 004–012

The queue is ordered by information value and feasibility. It deliberately prioritizes evidence that can establish a marginal resource-to-outcome relationship over additional descriptive program material.

| Priority | Cases | Acquisition target | Why it matters | Promotion gate unlocked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 004, 005 | FTE/officer-hour or service-hour exposure by period and location | defines the actual marginal intervention | resource → capacity → activity |
| 2 | 009, 010 | site/component-level enforcement exposure plus collision records | supports candidate-specific comparison | outcome + serious-harm linkage |
| 3 | 011, 012 | staffing/vehicle/service-unit exposure and response/care outcomes | creates a production relationship | capacity → outcome |
| 4 | 007, 008 | participant/contact exposure, eligibility and comparison cohorts | avoids program-level ecological attribution | causal identification |
| 5 | 006 | ANCHOR call-level operations, dispatch category, disposition and matched comparison | establishes a current-decision evidence plane | counterfactual + transportability |

| 7 | 004–012 | geography, shift, call type and baseline-risk distributions | tests transportability | transportability + correlated uncertainty |
| 8 | 004–012 | pre-specified alternative interventions using same marginal unit | prevents isolated optimization | alternatives |

## Acquisition rule

A source that only reports total spending, total staffing, total calls, or citywide outcomes is retained as contextual evidence unless it can be linked to the candidate exposure. Totals must not be divided into per-unit effects without an explicit allocation denominator and production assumption.

## Temporal rule

For the frozen RC1 reconstruction, only evidence admissible as of `2023-12-06` may enter the historical plane. Later material is stored as `CURRENT_LEARNING` and can support a new decision context only.

## Stop condition

Do not open a recommendation merely because a case has a long evidence file. Promotion requires the complete causal chain, candidate-specific counterfactual, serious-harm pathway, transportability, uncertainty/sensitivity, alternatives, and reproducible lineage.
