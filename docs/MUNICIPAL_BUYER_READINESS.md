# VIDIK municipal buyer readiness

## Buyer proposition

VIDIK is intended to help a municipality make scarce public-safety resource decisions more transparently by connecting local context to admissible evidence, uncertainty, alternatives, governance and later outcome review.

It is **decision support**, not an autonomous authority to allocate public resources.

## Primary municipal users

- executive/public-safety decision owners;
- analysts and policy staff;
- finance/budget staff evaluating marginal resource choices;
- program owners responsible for interventions;
- governance, privacy, records and procurement reviewers;
- technical/data teams responsible for municipal source connections.

## What a pilot delivers

1. Decision definition and constraints.
2. Municipal data/source reconciliation.
3. Evidence and parameter provenance package.
4. Canonical decision analysis.
5. Sensitivity/uncertainty/VOI challenge layer.
6. Alternatives and counterfactual record.
7. Tamper-evident decision artifact.
8. Governance/override record.
9. Outcome-learning review plan.

## What a buyer must receive before procurement approval

### Technical

- architecture and deployment model;
- data-flow diagram;
- supported source inventory;
- API/integration contract;
- availability and recovery targets;
- backup/restore evidence;
- security assessment and penetration-test results.

### Privacy / records

- data classification;
- privacy impact assessment support;
- retention/deletion schedule;
- access-control model;
- audit-log policy;
- data residency/hosting statement;
- incident-response process.

### Evidence / decision integrity

- causal evidence provenance;
- transportability/admissibility rules;
- uncertainty and sensitivity methodology;
- recommendation-flip explanation;
- VOI methodology;
- artifact integrity/replay method;
- explicit limitations and non-claims.

### Commercial

- pricing and packaging;
- implementation scope;
- support/SLA;
- renewal/termination terms;
- customer-success ownership;
- procurement/legal terms;
- insurance and liability position as applicable.

## Pilot decision rule

A pilot may demonstrate the product before all enterprise controls are complete, but production public-sector use must not be represented as certified until deployment, security, privacy, governance and operational gates are independently evidenced.

## Positioning guardrails

Do not claim:

- that VIDIK itself proves causality from municipal data;
- that a similarity score makes evidence causal;
- that synthetic marginal-resource scenarios are real-world effects;
- that repository CI equals production security certification;
- that the current repository already exposes a hosted public API;
- that a recommendation is an instruction to government rather than decision support.
