# VIDIK

VIDIK is a jurisdiction-portable decision-intelligence platform for evidence-constrained resource, investment, operational, development and public-interest decisions.

## Decision model

VIDIK starts with the problem rather than a predetermined intervention, establishes the status quo, discovers the relevant option universe, connects evidence to claims and parameters, evaluates marginal effects and constraints, exposes uncertainty and opportunity cost, records human overrides, and preserves an auditable decision artifact for outcome review and learning.

## Product audiences

- **Municipal / government** — public-sector resource allocation, service, safety, housing, health and environmental decisions.
- **Business / enterprise** — operating, investment, procurement, portfolio, expansion and risk decisions.
- **Developer / project** — site selection, project scope, phasing, infrastructure and community-impact decisions.
- **Research / analysis** — reproducible evidence, scenario, counterfactual and transferability analysis.
- **Public / community** — transparent, challengeable public-interest decisions.

The evidence boundary is deliberate: when a validated evidence chain or live adapter is missing, VIDIK blocks or marks the decision as unresolved rather than inventing a recommendation.
