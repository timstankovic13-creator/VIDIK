## Release discipline

A passing offline gate means the deterministic software layer is internally coherent. It does **not** mean VIDIK has proven a real-world causal effect or earned a municipal recommendation.

No recommendation is promoted solely because a dataset was found. Real recommendations require the experiment contract, temporal admissibility, authorized exposure, causal design/comparator, measurement readiness, and the applicable decision gates.
