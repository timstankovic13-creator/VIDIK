# VIDIK $5M Public Safety Decision Lab

## Purpose
A synthetic, reproducible allocation laboratory for a hypothetical $5,000,000 marginal public-safety pool. It is explicitly **not** a municipal recommendation and cannot populate production effect estimates, ROI, or recommendation fields.

## What it tests
- constrained allocation and capacity limits;
- status-quo comparison and opportunity-cost reasoning;
- risk ceilings;
- sensitivity / recommendation-flip analysis;
- uncertainty and evidence-gap disclosure;
- Why / Why-not explanation;
- explicit separation of modeled assumptions from observed evidence.

## Scenarios
Ten known-answer synthetic scenarios are stored in `data/VIDIK_PUBLIC_SAFETY_5M_SCENARIOS.json` and executed by `js/vidik-public-safety-5m-lab.js`.

## Rule
Synthetic values may test the mechanics of allocation. They must never be presented as measured municipal effect sizes or real ROI.
