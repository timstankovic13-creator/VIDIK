# VIDIK Value Benchmark

Measure whether VIDIK improves decision quality rather than merely producing faster prose.

## Metrics
- time from question to decision-ready package;
- material evidence gaps detected;
- unsupported causal claims prevented;
- appropriate `NO RECOMMENDATION` decisions;
- opportunity costs surfaced;
- sensitivity/flip thresholds identified;
- audit completeness and reproducibility;
- human overrides captured with rationale;
- outcome-review completeness;
- decisions changed because of newly acquired material evidence.

## Comparison
Run matched cases through a conventional-analysis baseline and VIDIK. Report paired results and preserve the distinction between process improvement and actual public-safety impact.
