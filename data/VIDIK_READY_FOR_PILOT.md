# VIDIK Ready-for-Pilot Gate

VIDIK is ready to enter a controlled pilot when the local readiness layer is complete and the customer can supply a real decision with an agreed baseline and measurable outcomes.

It is **not** required to wait for every public-data causal experiment to become estimable. A pilot can begin with recommendation, decision-support and fail-closed cases, provided the claim level is explicit.

The pilot must not be marketed as proof of causal effectiveness until the relevant exposure, comparator and measurement gates are passed.
