# VIDIK Customer Decision Package v1

## Executive page

- Decision question
- Decision deadline
- Recommended action, or `NO RECOMMENDATION`
- Status-quo comparison
- Expected benefit / relevant outcome
- Confidence and key uncertainty
- Top trade-off
- What would change the answer

## Decision rationale

- Why this option
- Why not the leading alternatives
- Resource requirement and marginal unit
- Evidence quality and causal basis
- Sensitivity / recommendation-flip conditions

## Evidence page

- Evidence claims and source provenance
- Temporal admissibility
- Transportability and conflicts
- MSE gaps and acquisition status
- Explicit distinction between descriptive, decision-support and effect-estimation claims

## Model page

- Causal/production chain
- Parameters and uncertainty
- Counterfactual/status quo
- Alternatives
- VOI where material
- Assumptions and constraints

## Governance page

- Decision Object identity/version
- Human decision and override rationale
- Integrity checks
- Audit snapshot
- Access/tenant metadata as applicable

## Outcome plan

- Prediction to monitor
- 6-month, 1-year, 2-year and 5-year checkpoints where applicable
- Outcome owner
- Review trigger
- Recalibration/drift handling

## Product rule

The package is generated from the Decision Object. No customer-facing report may contain a stronger claim than the underlying admissibility and causal gates support.
