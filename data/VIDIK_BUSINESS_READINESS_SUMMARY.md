# VIDIK Business Readiness Summary

## Built now

- executable decision benchmark;
- canonical end-to-end lifecycle;
- canonical municipal demo workflow;
- customer decision package contract;
- municipal adapter contract and portability test;
- adversarial/reproducibility gate;
- pilot measurement framework;
- commercial readiness gate;
- October CI release discipline.

## Not yet proven

- real municipal operational-data access for blocked causal cases;
- real customer turnaround improvement;
- production-scale customer security/procurement acceptance;
- October CI-green release candidate.

## Business transition criterion

The next proof is not another synthetic feature. It is a controlled pilot showing that VIDIK can take a real municipal decision, reach a defensible answer or useful block quickly, preserve an audit trail, and measurably reduce decision-cycle burden.
