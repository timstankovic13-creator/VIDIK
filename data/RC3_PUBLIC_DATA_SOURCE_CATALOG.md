## Evidence boundary

The City confirms monthly ASE speed KPIs are published and reports pilot-site changes in speed-limit compliance/high-end speeding. The public catalogue confirms site-level violation, collision-location and traffic-volume infrastructure.

These sources establish public-data availability. They do **not** establish authorized marginal resource exposure or a defensible untreated counterfactual for serious-harm outcomes. VIDIK therefore remains fail-closed for effect, ROI and recommendation until those gates are independently evidenced.

Historical decision boundary: `2023-12-06` (immutable).
