# RC3 Ottawa public-data execution — 2026-09-02

## Result

Cases 009 (Automated Speed Enforcement) and 010 (Red Light Camera) have sufficient public source infrastructure to proceed to data ingestion, but public data alone do not establish the two execution gates that VIDIK is required to protect:

1. authorized marginal intervention exposure attributable to the candidate resource allocation; and
2. a defensible untreated/comparison counterfactual for downstream safety outcomes.

Therefore no effect estimate, ROI, or recommendation is generated.

## 009 — ASE

Verified public source families:
- camera locations
- monthly speed metrics (average speed, 85th percentile, compliance, high-end speeding)
- historical camera violations 2020–2025
- post-removal monitoring speed data
- collision-by-location data for 2017–2024 excluding 2023
- intersection traffic volumes 2024
- midblock traffic volumes 2024

The City states that monthly speed KPIs are published and that the original pilot sites moved from 16% compliance before implementation to 57% at three months, 69% at one year, and 81% after three years. Those are program/site observations, not by themselves a causal serious-harm estimate.

## 010 — Red Light Camera

Verified public source families:
- camera locations
- annual/site violation datasets 2015–2026
- collision-by-location data for 2017–2024 excluding 2023
- intersection traffic volumes 2024

## Boundary decision

Public acquisition: PASS.

Marginal authorized exposure: BLOCKED.

Defensible counterfactual: BLOCKED.

Measurement readiness for causal serious-harm estimation: BLOCKED pending those gates.

Historical decision boundary remains 2023-12-06 and cannot be mutated by these later observations.

## Next required evidence

The minimum operational request is now explicit: obtain site-level treatment activation/deactivation dates, actual enforcement exposure/uptime by site-month, and an admissible comparison design; for collisions obtain date/site/severity records and enough traffic exposure to model outcome risk. Until that evidence is available, VIDIK must remain fail-closed.
