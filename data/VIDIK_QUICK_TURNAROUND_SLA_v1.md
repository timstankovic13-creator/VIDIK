# VIDIK Quick-Turnaround Operating SLA v1

## Standard target

- Day 0: intake and question lock.
- Day 1: evidence scan and MSE determination.
- Day 2: model/counterfactual construction.
- Day 3: analysis, governance QA and customer decision package.
- Day 5: outer bound for unusually complex evidence acquisition.

## Clock discipline

The clock measures elapsed customer decision time, not hidden analyst work. Pause the clock only for explicitly documented customer/municipal data dependencies or authorization decisions.

## Output SLA

Every engagement receives either:

1. a defensible recommendation with uncertainty and alternatives; or
2. a useful `NO RECOMMENDATION` / `INCONCLUSIVE / DATA FAILURE` explaining the blocking gate and minimum next evidence.

Speed never lowers the evidence threshold.
