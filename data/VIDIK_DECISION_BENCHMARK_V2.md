# VIDIK Decision Benchmark v2

The benchmark contains 10 synthetic municipal decision scenarios designed to have known behavioral expectations rather than fake empirical evidence. Required outcomes include: clear selection, near-tie sensitivity, risk-constrained exclusion, capacity saturation, fragile winner detection, no-admissible-candidate refusal, minimum-commitment handling, opportunity-cost recognition, and equity-constraint awareness.

A benchmark pass means VIDIK follows the specified decision semantics—not that the synthetic assumptions are true in the real world.

Expansion target: 20–30 scenarios before October certification, including adversarial evidence, temporal inadmissibility, confounding, spillover, missing exposure, correlated uncertainty, and misleading aggregates.
